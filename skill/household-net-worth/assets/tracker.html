Kids forecast

—

Plan each child by birth year. Enter what each category costs per month in today’s dollars and the ages it applies to. The forecast checks the child’s age month by month (from the birth month) and adds the months up to each year; the defaults are a nanny at age 0, daycare from 1 to 5, extracurricular activities from 4 and education from 6 to 17. Each category grows at its own inflation rate below. 529 contributions are savings: they stay in your net worth and grow at the 529 return below. College is not modeled yet.
